## game stability
when starting to drag a card, a networked copy appears in the middle of the table and updates when the card has been placed.
this is not as expected, the networked object should only appear when the card has been placed on the table.
players might consider playing a card but dont actually do so, other players can then see what card that player has in had.
additionally occasionally when drawing cards from the deck, they dont actually get added to the hand but also are missing from the deck.
